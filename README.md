# Go × City Game

A cozy isometric valley-builder about **two communities building together**. You are the mayor of Hearth; the CPU is the mayor of Harbor. You're both trying to maximize the valley's shared effort — but you never fully understand each other's plans, so cooperation takes real second-guessing.

Play on a 20×20 valley of 400 plots. Terrain, hills, and valleys are randomized every game, roads lay themselves, and seasons and weather roll through.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser — no build, no server, no dependencies.

## What's in the valley

- **Two mayors, one valley** — Hearth and Harbor build together with intentionally incomplete signals about each other's intentions; combined effort is the score
- **Water matters** — waterfront access attracts growth, but heavy rains can flood low plots and summer can bring inland drought
- **Farm-to-commerce food chain** — harvests flow from farms through connected networks: farmers markets serve rural communes, marketing agencies boost trade in population centers
- **Farm homesteads** — isolated houses and otherwise-alone neighboring pairs work as farmsteads with extra harvest and growth
- **Rural roads** — chains of same-community houses within two plots gradually grow roads linking them after a season
- **Trackside depots** — seasoned houses next to the rails develop into depots; when the weekly train passes, they distribute bonus goods through their connected rural communities
- **Weekly goods train** — every six turns a freight train circles the valley delivering crates to every settled plot in both communities, boosting area-wide wellbeing for the new week
- **Two cultures** — Italianate Hearth architecture vs Moroccan Harbor, each with detailed arches, shutters, balconies, roof tiles, patterned bands, and crenellated towers
- **Living places** — hillside villas, village centers, farming communes, and plazas evolve as communities mature
- **People with jobs** — farmers, cow herders, shopkeepers, and bicyclists (35% of connected riders become cargo cycle couriers running walk-in trade and delivery routes)
- **Wellness-driven growth** — per-tile wellness and quality of life drive growth; gardens bloom on the healthiest plots; hospitals, spiritual places, libraries, and parks emerge in mature, road-linked communities
- **Changing seasons and weather**, rendered at true 2× resolution with a custom isometric valley favicon

Built as a single self-contained `index.html` (+ favicon).
